# R-M2-101 and R-M2-102, investigated: what the contract requires, and what it never decided

Read-only, at `8bf8855`. No code changed, nothing rerun, no mailbox contacted, the Mac `.venv`
untouched. Sources are the architecture, the contract, the rubric, the shipped code and the
real-model trace `benchmarks/trace-first-response-ba99c7a.json` already on disk.

The checkpoint (`docs/reviews/M2_CHECKPOINT_2026-09-15.md` §3) named one question as the
smallest next step: *does a pool probe's result set belong in `H` as hits, or as accounted
members under a non-hit reason?* That question is answered below, and it was the wrong shape.
Both halves of the answer are contract findings rather than implementation ones, and the
second one (R-M2-102) turns out to be a deliberate prohibition rather than an oversight, which
corrects the checkpoint's suggested repair.

---

## 1. R-M2-101: the probe ids must be in `H`, and nothing decided they should be evidence

### 1.1 They must be in `H`, and step (b) was decided explicitly.

A.7a's clause (H-lex) opens generally - *"every ID returned by every executed Gmail
`messages.list` - on the pages actually fetched - at **any** rung"* - and then enumerates,
naming *"the **L5 pool-construction participant probes of D.5 step (b)**, which are
`messages.list` calls like any other and are covered by this clause (ADV-109)"*.
`PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md` I-1 (lines 288-294) and `RELEASE_RUBRIC.md` EV-01 (lines 191-194) carry
the same words, and D.5 itself devotes a headed rule to it: *"Pool membership is not disclosure
membership, and step (b) is not exempt from `H`"*, with the disposition spelled out - *"disclosed
if their thread is mapped, otherwise a `withheld` record with `cap: "max_pool_threads"` and a
pool-widening affordance"*. The anti-deflation rationale sits beside it: the shortlist's size is
a published constant *"so the size of `H` cannot be shrunk to make this invariant pass"*.

**So no repair may remove these ids from `H`**, and the question this investigation was given -
hits or non-hit members of `H` - is malformed, because in this architecture `H` *is* the hit set
(`disposition.py:1704`, `"""H."""`).

Note what the disposition rule those four documents agree on actually says: *disclosed if their
thread is mapped, otherwise withheld*. It is a rule about **accounting**. None of them says a
pool-probe id is an evidence-tier row at `body_clean` with a non-negotiable reply-chain floor.

### 1.2 The probe that does the work is step (c), and no clause anywhere analyses it.

D.5 builds the pool from three sources: *"(a) threads already touched by L0-L3; (b) threads of
participants parsed from the query (`from:`/`to:` probes, 5 u each); (c) most-recent threads in
the query's date window, or a default recency window if none."* Step (a) sends no probe (it is
the ladder's own touched threads). Step (b) is the one A.7a, I-1, EV-01, D.5 and ADV-109 all
name. **Step (c) is named nowhere in any of the `H` analyses.**

It is a `messages.list` call, so H-lex's general opening covers it and its ids are in `H`. But on
every one of the seven traced cases the query names no participant, so **step (b) sends nothing
and step (c) is the only probe that fires**: `probes_sent` is 1 on all seven, `by_step` carries
only `ladder` and `recency`, and the probe is `after:<epoch>` over the default 90-day window
(`semantic/pool.py:115-132`, `DEFAULT_RECENCY_DAYS`). That single probe admits **334 to 462 ids**
against a shortlist of 25.

> **Qualified 2026-09-16 — R-M2-110. The record above is preserved unchanged; this is what it
> now means.** Every figure in this project that counts what D.5 step (c)'s probe admitted was
> produced through `SyntheticMailbox`, whose `_date_value` matched `YYYY/MM/DD` only. The
> server writes that probe as `after:<epoch>` (A.6a rule 4), the double returned "cannot judge"
> for it, and "cannot judge" is implemented as **matching every message**. So the probe had no
> window in any run recorded here — **including the real-model runs**: the models decide which
> pool rows the shortlist selects and in what order, not whether the double filters by date.
> Real models did not make the synthetic date filtering correct.
>
> What survives: the **mechanism**. A pool probe's `messages.list` admits its whole result set
> to `H` at L5, and A.9 then spends the evidence tier and the E2 floor on it. That is a reading
> of the code and the contract and is unaffected.
>
> What does not survive: the **magnitude**. "334 to 462 ids" is a windowless probe's result set,
> not the probe the server sends to Gmail. Re-measured under the corrected double, the same
> mechanism leaves three demoted ids inside mapped threads on DIAG-SEM-03, not hundreds
> (`docs/reviews/A16_RECENCY_POOL_2026-09-15.md` §5.2, ledger R-M2-112). The size of this
> finding is unestablished until it is re-measured, and no work should be justified by the
> old number.

So the ids driving the cost enter `H` under the general half of a clause whose worked examples,
adversarial record and rubric text are all about a different probe.

### 1.3 And A.9 then spends the evidence budget on them.

A.9 spends the budget on `H` directly:

> 1. **Evidence tier.** Every hit at `body_clean`, subject to the A.9a ladder.
> 2. **Reply-chain floor (E2) - non-negotiable in *membership*.** Each hit's reply parent and
>    direct children are **always present in the response** [...] never dropped to make room.

and the code follows literally: `plan.py:375-376` gives `Band.EVIDENCE` to every
`message_id in thread.hit_ids`, and `plan.py:278` computes A.9(2)'s obligations with
`evidence_ids=thread.hit_ids`. So a message that fell inside a 90-day window is an evidence-tier
row, a candidate for A.9a step 3's top-k protection, and the anchor of a floor the ladder may
never drop.

**A.7a's accounting rule and A.9's representation rule were written for different purposes and
their composition was never argued.** A.7a says nothing may vanish without a disposition; A.9's
"every hit" was written when a hit meant a message the caller's query matched.

**The architecture has already drawn this line once, in D.5 itself.** The bullet immediately
above the step (b) rule: *"The pool creates **no `source`, no mini-map and no stub rows**"* -
A.9(5)'s "pool membership is not a source". Pool membership was carved out of one representation
obligation, on exactly the reasoning that applies to this one, and the carve-out stopped there.

### 1.4 What it costs, measured

The consequence is the concentration the diagnosis saw and mis-attributed. With the backend on,
the rank-0 lexical source's present hits go 4 to 57 (DIAG-RANK-03), 5 to 56 (SEM-03), 2 to 13
(REV-03) and 3 to 8 (EXP-03), while the shortlist puts 0, 20, 0 and 4 messages in those threads
respectively. **The shortlist is not what inflates them**; step (c)'s window is. That is why a
bag-of-words stand-in reproduced the real models exactly, before and after the A15 repair: the
model chooses 25 of about 300 scored rows, and that choice touches almost nothing the ladder
measures.

### 1.5 The remedy is available and the data for it is already recorded

`HitOrigin` (`disposition.py:784-800`) carries, per id, the `clause`, the `endpoint`, the
`rung` and the `query` that admitted it. So "admitted only by an L5 pool probe" is a fact the
ledger already holds per message; no new bookkeeping is needed to act on it. The product also
already has the vocabulary for the distinction: `ReasonKind.RECENCY_CONTEXT` beside
`GMAIL_QUERY_MATCH` and `SEMANTIC_SCORE`, and `Role.CONTEXT`, which the LR freshness rung
already uses for the rows it surfaces.

**This is an owner decision at the architecture level, not an implementation fix**, because the
literal text of A.9(1) and A.9(2) says "every hit". The decision is whether A.9's evidence tier
and E2 floor are owed to every member of `H`, or to the members the query selected - the lexical
matches and the shortlist's 25 - with pool-probe admissions carried as accounted context,
disposed of exactly as D.5 already specifies and never disclosed as evidence. **The narrowest
version is confined to step (c)**, the recency probe, which is the one no clause analyses and
the one doing the work on every traced case; step (b)'s participant probes could keep their
current treatment and the finding's cost would still go, because on these queries step (b)
sends nothing. If taken, it changes no
budget, no model, no corpus and no cap, leaves `|H|` and `certify` untouched, and is the only
identified change that would reduce the semantic arm's first-response cost at its source.

Two consequences to settle with it, named here and not resolved: `_carries_nothing` currently
reads "evidence survived" against `hit_ids` and would have to read the narrowed set, or a
response carrying only recency-window ids would count as carrying evidence; and `hit_threads`
ranks threads by the rung that admitted them, so a thread reached only by a pool probe would
need a stated place in that order.

## 2. R-M2-102: the score cannot order lexical threads, and that is ADV-110

The observation stands as recorded: on DIAG-RANK-03 the real embedding put the five highest
cosines in the pool and 24 of 25 shortlisted messages in the evidence thread `t-t0048`, and the
response mapped `t-t0004` and wrote `t-t0048` as a `max_hit_threads` group at rank 13, one place
ahead of where `sem-off` wrote it.

**The cause is a deliberate prohibition with a recorded reason, not a gap.** `hit_threads`
(`assemble.py:494-508`) guards the semantic key by rung:

> The semantic rank applies inside L5 and nowhere else, and the first version's safety argument
> for that was false. It said no lexical thread appears in `semantic_order` - but D.5's pool
> step (a) *seeds the pool with the threads L0-L3 touched*, so lexical threads are shortlisted
> routinely, and the stage-A cosine was deciding which lexical hits `max_hit_threads` mapped.
> That is a comparison the cosine cannot make: the threads the shortlist's `k` cut off have no
> cosine at all, which is ADV-110's mixed scale one layer out.

and A.9/D.7 state the rule the guard implements: *"a reused stage-A cosine may affect ordering
**only where it exists for every candidate in that comparison**; otherwise `ordering_effect:
false` [...] Mixing scales inside one comparison is the fabricated comparability RANK-03 exists
to prevent"* (ADV-110, HIGH).

`t-t0048` was admitted by L1b, so it is ranked as a lexical thread among twelve other lexical
threads that carry no cosine. Ordering it by its cosine is precisely what ADV-110 forbids.

**This corrects the checkpoint.** §3 offered, as the next lever if R-M2-101 came back "hits by
contract", *"give the score a path into the mapping decision by making `max_hit_threads` a
prefix of the ranking rather than of the retrieval order"*. That is not available: under ADV-110
it would require a cosine for every candidate thread, which means scoring every hit-bearing
thread rather than a bounded pool - a scope and cost change to D.5, not a reordering. R-M2-099's
open question (what `max_hit_threads` names) is unaffected and stays open; it is about which
prefix, not about scoring.

**What follows for measurement, and it is the load-bearing consequence.** The semantic rung can
change which threads are *mapped* only by adding threads no lexical rung reached; within the
lexical set it may not reorder. So on this corpus a semantic benefit can appear only on a case
whose evidence thread is **L5-only**. DIAG-RANK-03's is not: the embedding identified it
correctly and the architecture correctly refused to let that matter. **No case in the current
diagnostic set can demonstrate the rung's value even in principle**, and that is a property of
the case set, not of the rung. It is a requirement on the independently authored F1-F17 file,
not a code change.

## 3. What this changes

* R-M2-101 is restated as a **contract composition** finding with a documented precedent
  (A.9(5)) and an available remedy that does not touch `H`. Owner decision.
* R-M2-102 is restated as **working as designed under ADV-110**, with the repair the checkpoint
  suggested ruled out, and with a case-design requirement in its place.
* `M2_CHECKPOINT_2026-09-15.md` §3 and §4 are corrected accordingly.
* Nothing in the A15 repair is reopened, and neither finding affects it: A.9a step 7's last
  resort collapses the source whatever admitted its hits.
