# FINDINGS_LEDGER.md — every finding ever raised

**Authority:** `AGENT_LOOP.md` §4 (finding schema), §9 (records), §5 step 5 (consolidation).
**Maintained by:** the orchestrator, from reviewer reports. An implementer may not edit any
severity field, and no finding is ever deleted — a closed finding keeps its row and gains
closing evidence. A MEDIUM deferred by the orchestrator stays **open** with a note naming
the round it returns in.

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| OPEN | Raised, not yet fixed. |
| FIXED — AWAITING VERIFICATION | The implementer claims a fix. Not closed: a claim is a hypothesis (`AGENT_LOOP.md` §1). |
| CLOSED | A reviewer re-verified it in a named round, with the reproduction recorded. |
| DEFERRED | MEDIUM or LOW, explicitly scheduled by the orchestrator to a named round. Still open. |
| WAIVED | Human decision only, with the reason recorded here (`AGENT_LOOP.md` §10). |

## Severity

`BLOCKER` / `HIGH` / `MEDIUM` / `LOW` exactly as defined in `AGENT_LOOP.md` §4. BLOCKER and
HIGH are round-blocking; the round gate in §5 step 6 requires zero of each, open.

## Regression obligation

`RELEASE_RUBRIC.md` REG-01/REG-02: every closed BLOCKER or HIGH must name a regression test
derived from the actual failure, and that test must be demonstrated to **fail** against the
pre-fix commit. The `Regression test` column is where that test is named; a closed
BLOCKER/HIGH row with an empty column is itself a REG-01 violation.

## Round 18 — the rule is right, the predicate asks how it is spelled

Round 18 delivered OD-5's schema change and met the owner's fourth requirement outright: negated
phrases are exclusions. Evidence preservation held over 2,200 trials with 267 withheld records;
recall over 1,200 trials missed no thread; the suite has **zero** `f(x) == f(x)` self-comparing
assertions, and the implementer caught two vacuous tests it had written itself.

But A9-A2 required the asymmetry be derived from **what an operator does**, so it holds for scope
operators nobody has added yet — and `declares_the_search_region` derives it from **how one is
spelled**, `token.startswith("in:")`. `OperatorKind.LOCATION` already exists as declared data in
the operator registry and the predicate does not read it. R-RETR proved the consequence the only
way that counts: it registered a new `mailbox:` operator *as an implementer would*, with
`OperatorKind.LOCATION`, and **2,274 tests stayed green** while `-mailbox:spam` was dropped from
every decomposition unit, relaxed away at L2, and widened out of at L3 with spam enabled. 60 of

### Reading this list

- **The gate changed after round 14, and why.** Rounds 12-14 closed twelve findings, and the
  reviewers who filed them described **nineteen** as *"inert today"*, *"no consumer exists"*,
  *"nothing destructive exists yet"*, *"not reachable via any path"*. `envelope/` was touched by
  eleven review rounds; `retrieval/` by one. Round 11's R-SEC wrote, in as many words, *"Don't

  advice to fix immediately. Rounds 12, 13 and 14 followed from that misreading.
  The old gate — *no open finding* — has no fixed point when adversarial reviewers are pointed at
  in-process Python objects: there is always another reconstruction path and always another claim
  slightly wider than its code. The gate is now **no finding reachable from code that exists**

  that would make it reachable, and returns there with its reproduction. Nothing is dropped;
  severity is never downgraded to make this true.

**What R-M2-069 and R-M2-070 mean, and it is not a bug list.** The first three rounds found
things a generator had put there and could take away. These two are properties of *generating
the text at all*: a generator that writes a decisive sentence differently from an ordinary one
leaves a register signal, and a generator that lays out a family the same way twice leaves a
shape signal. They can be attacked - vary the layout, write evidence in hedged registers
sometimes, plant unhedged assertions that are wrong - but each of those weakens the case the
family is registered to make, and the next read will measure whatever regularity replaces them.

The useful response is to stop treating a query-free solver's score as a defect to be driven to
zero and start treating it as **a baseline the campaign publishes**. It belongs in the report
next to H1, H2 and H3, and it is computable from the corpus before any mailbox is touched.

**What the difference between the two scores does and does not license.** An earlier draft of
this paragraph said that an arm scoring 30 of 39 against a query-free solver's 24 of 39 has
"demonstrated six cases' worth of retrieval". That is wrong and is withdrawn. The two arms are
not nested, the cases they get right are not the same cases, and a difference of aggregate
scores is not a count of cases solved by the mechanism under test. What the baseline licenses
is a **paired, case-level** comparison: which cases each arm answered, where they disagree, and
in which direction - reported per family, with the budget stated. An aggregate margin on its
own establishes nothing about mechanism and must not be reported as if it did.
